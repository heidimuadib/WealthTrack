WealthTrack Privacy Policy

Effective 2 August 2026 · Last updated 2 August 2026

WealthTrack is a personal expense tracker. You record what you spend, and the app shows you where it went. This policy describes exactly what the app stores, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

The short version. WealthTrack stores the expenses you type in, the categories and budgets you set, and the account details you signed up with. It has no access to any bank, card or financial institution. It shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

What is collected

Account information

  • Your email address, used to identify your account and sign you in.
  • A password, if you created the account with one. It is stored only as a bcrypt hash — the original is never written down and cannot be recovered from what is stored.
  • A Google account identifier, if you signed in with Google. WealthTrack receives your Google account's identifier, email address and name. It does not receive your Google password and cannot act on your Google account.
  • A display name, if you set one.
  • A profile photo, if you upload one.
  • The dates your account was created and last updated.

Financial information you enter

  • Expense amounts, dates, and any notes you attach.
  • Categories you create, including their names and colours.
  • Monthly budget amounts.

All of this is information you type in yourself. WealthTrack does not connect to any bank, card issuer or financial institution, does not import transactions, and has no ability to move money.

Technical information

  • An authentication token issued when you sign in, which expires after seven days. It identifies your session and nothing else.
  • Your device stores that token along with your name, email and profile photo address, plus your language and appearance preferences and the category you last used, so the app can start without asking again.

WealthTrack contains no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers. It does not collect your location, contacts, or the contents of your device.

Where it is stored

  • Account, expense, budget and category records are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase.
  • The WealthTrack server runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Profile photos are stored as files on the WealthTrack server, not in the database. Each is given an unpredictable filename. Anyone holding that exact address can view the image without signing in, in the same way a photo served from a content delivery network can be. Photo addresses are not listed or searchable, and no other part of your data can be reached this way.
  • The token and cached profile described above are held on your own device and are removed when you log out or delete your account.

What is never done

  • Your personal information is never sold.
  • It is never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No advertising is shown in the app.
  • Your expense data is never used to profile or score you.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — used only if you choose to sign in with Google, to confirm the account is yours.
  • Supabase — hosts the database holding your records.
  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure — hosts the WealthTrack server.

These providers process data on WealthTrack's behalf in order to run the service. No other third party receives your information.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. If your account has a password you will be asked for it; if you signed up with Google you will be asked to type a confirmation word instead.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes:

  • every expense you have recorded;
  • every budget you have set;
  • every category you have created;
  • your profile photo file;
  • your account record, including your email, name and password hash.

There is no recovery period and no backup copy is kept for you to restore from. Once deleted, the data is gone and WealthTrack cannot retrieve it. Your session ends immediately and the token from it stops working.

Records are kept for as long as your account exists. WealthTrack does not retain a separate copy of your expense history after deletion. Routine infrastructure backups taken by the hosting providers may retain residual copies for a short period before being overwritten in the normal course of their operation.

Security

  • Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Every request for your data is checked against your own session, and every query is restricted to your account.
  • Sign-in and account-deletion attempts are rate limited.
  • Passwords and tokens are never written to server logs.

No system is perfectly secure, and WealthTrack cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

WealthTrack is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use. The app does not ask for a date of birth and performs no age verification. If you believe a child has created an account, use the contact address below and it will be removed.

Your choices

  • You can edit your display name and profile photo at any time in the app.
  • You can remove your profile photo without deleting your account.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data as described above.
  • You can request a copy of your data using the contact address below.
